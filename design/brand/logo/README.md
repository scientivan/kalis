# Kalis logo concepts (draft, not chosen)

Three concepts for one idea: an expert's sealed method meets the buyer's sealed data, and a verified answer comes out. Colours come from FRONTEND_GUIDE.md (blue #0038FF, ink #101010, white, soft blue #DCE1F5). Wordmark is Inter weight 800, letter spacing -1.2 at 40px, set as live text (outline it before final export).

Each folder has symbol.svg, wordmark.svg, lockup-horizontal.svg, lockup-reversed-blue.svg, favicon.svg, mono-black.svg and reversed-blue.svg.

| Concept | Shape | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Meeting K | black stem plus blue chevron, check at the join | Your data (stem) and the expert's method (arms) meet in one letter and the meeting point is a verified answer. The check disappears below 32px. |
| 2 Sealed pair | one closed block, blue half and black half, white check across the seam | Two sealed halves locked together produce a checked result without either opening. Strongest at 16px, but reads as a generic checkbox. |
| 3 Checked K | blue tile, K whose lower leg becomes a check | Name first, verified result second. Most ownable at small sizes, but tells less of the two-sided privacy story. |

Avoided on purpose: padlocks, shields, hexagons and keyholes (common across privacy and compute brands).
