## Observed reference

Intro uses a large tilted image, smaller overlapping framed photo and sticker on the left, centered copy on the right. Sharp rectangular split-arrow buttons and small navigation contrast the oversized display. Blue brand/client marks move horizontally. Motion includes pointer-responsive collages, masked type and scroll-driven stack transitions.

These two display faces intentionally replace the earlier all-Inter landing approach in order to follow Dien's new reference. Font licenses ship alongside the assets. No app styles change.

Technology and connector strips remain, but now use a compact editorial rhythm and blue identity marks. Status labels distinguish Sheets/Docs mock connectors from upcoming apps. Intro uses an original generated cobalt still-life, overlapping private cost sheet and a seal stamp. Methods use a pinned stack of large blue-framed product posters. Each is a program/input illustration with an actual app link, not a benchmark or outcome claim. Protections become three tilted interactive permission, note and outbound posters. Community content becomes four offset posters with real issues, contributions and proposal destinations. FAQ and large mixed-type invitation close the page.

Desktop stack responds directly to scroll. Mobile and reduced motion show all three methods vertically. Decorative loops pause offscreen, on hidden pages, on connector focus/hover and through the motion control. Body text and labels remain readable. The reference's black showcase and pink/orange borders are translated into white and blue to preserve Dien's palette constraint.
