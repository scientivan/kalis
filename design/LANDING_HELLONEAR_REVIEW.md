# HelloNear reference and Kalis adaptation

Source https://hellonear.xyz/ inspected 15 November 2026. HTML and current stylesheet fetched directly. Scope is landing sections below the hero. Hero assets are deferred by Dien.

## Reference observations

HelloNear separates spacious, large centered headings from asymmetric product demonstrations. The Minds section uses three portrait cards, slightly rotated with a deal entrance and hover lift. Payment and safety sections pair explanatory copy with concrete approval and blocked-payment interfaces. A graph visualizes connections. The later demo and final invitation change scale and pacing again. Its motion includes finite card deals, meaningful state changes, floating assets, marquee and graph exploration. These are observations of the reference, not Kalis capabilities.

## Adaptation

Keep Kalis Meeting K, Inter, white surfaces and app blue #0038ff. Do not copy HelloNear characters, yellow accent, brand assets, text or partnerships. Preserve the hero as requested. Replace the previous static lower-section layout with selectable method cards, input/output preview, consent illustration, connected two-party privacy diagram with inspector tabs, method-fee allocation illustration, contribution version preview, FAQ and final invitation. Illustrations explicitly do not send data or make payments. Payment allocation is an example of the method fee, with network/compute separate. No fabricated ratings or testimonials.

The full reference includes dark graph and landscape closing treatments. They are deliberately adapted to white backgrounds because Dien explicitly required white outside the hero. Animation can be paused, reduced motion disables automatic motion, and mobile uses a horizontally scrollable method deck. The original hero step carousel remains removed.

## User correction and second adaptation

Dien rejected the first adaptation because it omitted reference-defining mechanisms. The earlier interpretation was incomplete, not an approved match. Reinspection of actual page markup confirms the approval section has an ap-pin viewport, masked word heading, ap-visual clip-path from the right, a wide ap-stage scene, and a second safety scene. The technology and integration strips have duplicated marquee rows.

Implemented in Kalis after this correction. A desktop 260vh scroll section pins one viewport, wipes the scene from right to left, then pans from the permission scene to buyer checks. The opening copy is left-aligned in a constrained column, while the illustration becomes the dominant scene. The scene uses actual Kalis UI, a cost sheet and tender document represented as product objects. No HelloNear character is copied. Mobile, paused motion and reduced motion render scenes in normal reading order. Headings reveal per word through masks. Built with lists NEAR, NEAR AI, OutLayer and WebAssembly with their actual roles. A duplicated connector marquee uses the app catalog and brand paths already in the repository. Sheets and Docs carry Demo connector labels and the other names Coming soon. Source logos are CC0 Simple Icons as recorded in brand-icons.ts.

Motion is scroll-bound for the main scene and linear for the marquee. Loops pause offscreen, in hidden tabs, on marquee hover/focus and through the section motion control.
