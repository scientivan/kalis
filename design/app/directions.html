Three directions for the Kalis app

Each tile shows the palette, the type scale at app size, one real marketplace card, the primary button and the signature element. All three use the SF Pro system stack at weight 500 and above, solid colours and a textured background, as your design rules ask. Pick one, or combine parts.

The card content is real. The tender method, the demo seller name, the price of 0.1 NEAR testnet and "No ratings yet" come from the backend as it is now. Every seller in the demo is fictional and labelled as a demo.
